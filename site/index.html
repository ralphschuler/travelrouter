A router console.
Not another cloud account.

The phone in your hand is the control panel. The router works locally and carries its setup tools, packages and firmware in the image.

⌁

Start without Ethernet

Boot into a protected hotspot, replace the factory passwords and choose an upstream. Saved Wi-Fi networks reconnect automatically.

◇

Choose your privacy route

Configure WireGuard or HTTPS/SOCKS5 proxy tunnels. Protected modes block direct client traffic when the tunnel is unavailable.

≋

Own your DNS policy

Unbound caches and validates DNSSEC answers. Choose DNS-over-TLS or recursive resolution, with domain and country filters.

▦

See what is happening

A mobile-first dashboard for traffic, devices, system load, resolver statistics and local service logs.

↧

Keep your configuration

Download a password-encrypted backup with saved Wi-Fi and tunnel settings. Validate and restore it in the local panel.

⟳

Update deliberately

Signed application updates and optional Alpine stable package staging. Updates activate on reboot, when you choose.

From image to hotspot.

Back up your device before flashing. Setup requires replacing both shared factory passwords before the router forwards traffic.

Read the operations guide
  1. Verify and flash

    Download the image and SHA256SUMS from the release. Verify the checksum, then choose “Use custom” in Raspberry Pi Imager.

  2. Join the hotspot

    Connect to TravelRouter-XXXX with TravelRouter-Setup!. Open http://10.42.0.1/.

  3. Make it yours

    Sign in with ChangeMe-TravelRouter!. Replace the administrator and Wi-Fi passwords, reconnect, then select your upstream.
