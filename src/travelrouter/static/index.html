Privacy route

Checking privacy settings…

Kill-switch enabled in tunnel modes

Leave empty to reuse the saved tunnel. One full IPv4 tunnel is supported. Executable hooks and custom routes are rejected.

Leave the hostname empty to reuse the saved proxy. HTTPS CONNECT uses certificate validation and supports TCP; unsupported UDP is blocked.

Public Wi-Fi sign-in needed? Choose Direct, complete its sign-in, then re-enable your VPN or proxy. Protected modes never silently fall back to a direct client connection.

DNS resolution

Both modes validate DNSSEC and cache answers locally. DNS follows the protected route when one is active.

Recursive DNS avoids a central provider. Queries to authoritative servers are normally unencrypted.

Domain protection

Block known advertising, tracking, scam and malware domains.

Regional restrictions

One entry per line. Country blocks use IPdeny IPv4 allocation data and can affect shared CDNs. No countries are blocked by default.

Apps using their own encrypted DNS may bypass domain filtering. Country IP blocks still apply. IPv6 forwarding is disabled.
